INPUT
Creek video feed
IP camera fixed above the water surface, powered from the UPS HAT
Annotated dataset
dataset_v7: bottles, bags and food containers, split 70/10/20
Operator settings
Region of interest, accumulation threshold and alert preferences
PROCESS
Detection
YOLO26n, exported to NCNN, classifies each frame on the Raspberry Pi 5
Tracking and region filtering
ByteTrack keeps one identity per item so its box and label stay steady; detections outside the drawn water area are discarded
Accumulation counting
Items inside the area are counted each frame, smoothed over 15 frames and compared with the threshold, by default 20
Logging and alerting
Alerts, captures and their snapshots are written to SQLite and synchronised to Firebase
OUTPUT
Live annotated feed
Bounding boxes, labels and the running count on the touchscreen dashboard
Accumulation alert
On-screen warning, buzzer and a push notification to the Android application
Detection history
Searchable log with a saved snapshot for every recorded event
FEEDBACK
Evaluation against ISO/IEC 25010:2023, covering functional suitability, performance efficiency and usability, feeds back into retraining, threshold calibration and interface refinement.
